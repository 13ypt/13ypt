=== https://collections.ucl.ac.uk/Details/petrie/74235 [200]
Reproduction
Description
Roughly cut round-topped 'Horus stela' of diorite (?), incised with head of Bes above Horus as a human child facing right and standing on two crocodiles, the sides and undersides covered in crudely incised hieroglyphs giving Incantation X of the Mettemich stela, starting at the edge left of the figure then running down the edge right of the figure, ending on the back from right to left in three vertical columns. 'Hail god, son of a god; hail, heir son of an heir; hail, bull son of a bull...' Central top area has a groove between abraded edges, presumably the remains of a suspension loop.
