Ancient Egyptian Exhibition featuring objects from the IEAA Collection

Egyptian antiquities from the collection of the Institute of Egyptian Art and Archaeology. The collection spans the entire range of ancient Egyptian history and prehistory (ca. 100,000 B.C.E. through 700 C.E.). There are mummies, religious and funerary items, jewelry, sculpture, and objects from everyday life.
