Events This Week

  • Chasing Trane: The John Coltrane Documentary

    Chasing Trane: The John Coltrane Documentary (2017). Directed by John Scheinfeld. 1 hr. 39 min., documentary, NR; and a three-day youth jazz residency with JazzReach, February 24–26, 2027 In celebration of John Coltrane’s 100th birthday, the North Carolina Museum of Art brings his legacy home with events that celebrate his impact. Born in Hamlet and raised in High Point, Coltrane remains one of North Carolina’s most influential cultural figures and a transformative force in American music and global jazz. We start with a screening of Chasing Trane: The John Coltrane Documentary on September 23 and round out the celebration with a three-day youth jazz residency with JazzReach, February 24–26, 2027. The documentary is an exploration of the global power and impact of the music of Coltrane in which the passions, experiences, and forces that shaped his life and revolutionary sounds are revealed. Watch the trailer here. Through partnerships with area schools, the JazzReach residency introduces the next generation to Coltrane’s music, creativity, and spirit of exploration. Knightdale High School’s Jazz Band takes that legacy a step further, performing alongside JazzReach’s acclaimed Metta Quintet in a culminating concert. Together, these experiences connect North Carolina musical heritage to young people discovering their own voices as artists and musicians. Save the dates: February 24–26, 2027.

  • Public Tour

    Join us on a 50-minute tour of the People’s Collection. These tours are great during a first-time visit, after lunch at NCMA Café, or if you’re curious about the 5,000+ years of art and culture exhibited at the Museum. Tours vary and are subject to docent availability. NCMA docents may explore several galleries, focus on specific artists, or delve into the fundamentals and elements of art. All ages are welcome! To participate, meet your docent in West Building at the public tour sign.

    September 24, 2026

  • Craft Night at the Museum

    Crafting is more fun with friends! Come on by to make some art and new friends after hours at the Museum. Bring in any craft project you’re working on or create something fresh with provided art supplies. Each month we have a different suggested theme connected to our galleries and some artistic inspiration to bring back home. Grab refreshments at the Café, kick back, and meet some fellow crafters.

    September 24, 2026

  • Fall Service Project in the Park

    Space is limited, and preregistration is required. Help steward the Museum Park by supporting staff in their restoration initiatives. Volunteers plant native species around the Park to increase biodiversity and provide habitat for wildlife. Includes walking up to a mile total on paved and grassy surfaces with slight inclines as well as lifting, bending, and digging. Ages 14 and up. These projects occur twice a year, weather permitting, in fall and spring. Thank you to McAdams SHARES for sponsoring this event.

Winston-Salem

One Museum. Two Locations.

The NCMA W-S (formerly the Southeastern Center for Contemporary Art) in Winston-Salem, NC, was founded in 1956 and had been an affiliate of the NCMA since 2007. It was renamed the North Carolina Museum of Art, Winston-Salem, in fall 2023 by the North Carolina Legislature. 

NCMA W-S presents and interprets contemporary art of the United States with programs encompassing the issues engaging artists today.
