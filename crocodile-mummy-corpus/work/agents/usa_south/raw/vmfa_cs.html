The Collection

The Virginia Museum of Fine Arts houses a remarkable permanent collection of more than 50,000 works of art from almost every major world culture. Especially noteworthy are the museum’s collections of Art Nouveau, Art Deco, and Modern and Contemporary American art; French Impressionist and Post-Impressionist art and British sporting art; American art; Fabergé jeweled objects; and English Silver. The museum’s holdings of South Asian, Himalayan, and African art are among the finest in the nation.
